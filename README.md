# Consent Pickable

Take full control over item pickup with an immersive, interaction-based system that removes automatic vacuum pickup and gives players precise control over every item they collect.

## Features

### No Vacuum Pickup

* Items dropped on the ground stay exactly where they are until a player explicitly picks them up
* Works for every dropped item, including items that were already on the ground before the mod was installed

### Interactive HUD Prompt

* Aim at any dropped item to see its name, quantity and rarity, together with the available actions
* Only items in your line of sight are targeted: no picking items up through walls
* The prompt follows your crosshair smoothly and disappears as soon as you look away

### Tap to Pick Up

* Tap the **Use** key (**F** by default) to pick up the item you are looking at
* Existing inventory stacks are filled before new slots are used, keeping your inventory clean
* If your inventory is too full for the whole stack, you take what fits and the rest stays on the ground

### Hold to Swap

* Hold the **Use** key for **0.5 seconds** to swap the targeted item with the item in your active hotbar slot
* The item you were holding is safely dropped where the other item was, never deleted

### Collect All

* When several items lie close together, crouch and press **Use** to collect them all at once
* The prompt shows how many items are nearby
* Items are collected nearest first, until your inventory is full

### Smart Stack Top-Off

* Walking over an item still collects it automatically **only** if you already carry an incomplete stack of it
* It never takes new inventory slots and never goes beyond the stack limit
* Uses the game's normal pickup range and timing

## Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Show item information | Aim at a dropped item |
| Pick up the targeted item | **Use** key (F) |
| Swap with the active hotbar item | Hold **Use** for 0.5 s |
| Collect every nearby item | Crouch + **Use** |
| Top off an existing stack | Walk over the item |

Designed to make item collection more deliberate, readable and immersive while preserving fast inventory management.

## Languages

English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French and German.

## Compatibility

* Uses the game's own inventory rules, so pickups behave like vanilla pickups (stack limits, pickup sounds and notifications)
